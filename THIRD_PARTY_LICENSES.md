## amiga-boing-web

The source-derived geometry and animation model in `examples/boing_ball.rs` are
based on [amiga-boing-web](https://github.com/mbackschat/amiga-boing-web), a
port of the AMICUS Disk 9 Boing Ball variant. The terminal example omits its
Workbench layer and audio.
